## Meeting Rooms

### 1. Margaretha's Place
- **Location:** 2nd floor
- **Capacity:** 30-90 participants
- **Type:** Large room for classic meetings, lectures & associations
- **Technology:** Projector available
- **Half-day:** 14,400 SEK
- **Full-day:** 21,600 SEK

### 2. Sofia's Place
- **Location:** 2nd floor
- **Capacity:** Up to 26 people (can be divided)
- **Divisible:** Can split into 10 + 16 person rooms
- **Type:** Perfect for interviews, workshops, brainstorming
- **Technology:** Screen available
- **Pricing (Half-day):**
  - 26 people: 7,520 SEK
  - 16 people: 4,960 SEK
  - 10 people: 3,040 SEK
- **Pricing (Full-day):**
  - 26 people: 9,920 SEK
  - 16 people: 7,040 SEK
  - 10 people: 4,320 SEK

### 3. Sankt Hans Place
- **Location:** 2nd floor
- **Capacity:** Up to 18 people
- **Type:** Board meeting room, bright and comfortable
- **Technology:** Projector or Screen available
- **Half-day:** 3,800 SEK
- **Full-day:** 5,120 SEK

## Food & Beverage Pricing

**IMPORTANT: Food and catering are NOT included in conference room pricing. All catering arrangements must be made in advance.**

**Meals:**
- Place breakfast: 63 SEK per person
- Hot lunch: 210 SEK per person
- Package offer: 600 SEK per person (breakfast + coffee with pastries + hot lunch)

**Coffee/Refreshments:**
- Coffee with pastries: 62 SEK per person
- Coffee with cookies: 35 SEK per person

**Notes:**
- All prices exclude VAT
- Catering must be ordered in advance
- Custom catering arrangements available upon request
- Contact hotel for special dietary requirements and vegetarian/vegan options
