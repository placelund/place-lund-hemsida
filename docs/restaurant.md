## Restaurant - "A Place to Eat"

### Breakfast Buffet
- **Availability:** Complimentary for hotel guests ONLY
- **Service:** Daily
- **Format:** Hot and cold buffet

#### Breakfast Offerings
- Fresh bread and croissants
- Coffee, tea, and juice
- Hot items
- Cold items
- Vegetarian options available
- Vegan options available

**IMPORTANT:**
- Breakfast is ONLY included with standard hotel room bookings
- NOT included with studio or apartment stays

### Lunch & Dinner
- No lunch service currently offered
- No dinner service currently offered (except for hotel room guests - check-in included dinner)

---

## Bar - "A Place to Drink"

### Opening Hours
- **Daily:** 11:00 AM - 9:00 PM
- **Note:** May close earlier during periods of low occupancy

### Bar Offerings

#### Alcoholic Beverages
- Beer (various selections)
- Wine (red, white, rosé)
- Cider
- Cocktails

#### Non-Alcoholic Options
- Coffee
- Tea
- Alcohol-free beverages
- Soft drinks

### Special Features
- **Outdoor Seating:** Garden area available during summer months
- **Casual Visitors:** Coffee and tea available for non-guests
- **Relaxed Atmosphere:** Perfect for after-work drinks or casual meetings
