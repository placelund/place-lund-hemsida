## Group Stay Overview

Place Lund Hotel is ideal for hosting groups of various sizes and purposes, offering flexible accommodation options and comprehensive facilities.

### Capacity
- **Hotel Rooms:** Multiple configurations for different group sizes
- **Conference Facilities:** Up to 150 people across 3 meeting rooms
- **Restaurant & Bar:** Group dining arrangements available
- **Common Areas:** Conference lounge, breakout area with pool table

## Room Options for Groups

### Traditional Hotel Rooms (with meals)
- Single rooms (street or garden view)
- Twin rooms (2 beds, street or garden view)
- Economy double rooms (with or without patio)
- Double deluxe (2 rooms, up to 4 people)

### Hotel Studios (self-catering)
- Kitchenette for meal preparation
- Ideal for groups wanting flexibility
- **No meals included**
- Good for mixed group types

### Apartments (extended groups)
- Studio apartments (1-2 people)
- One/Two bedroom apartments (2-4 people)
- **No meals included**
- Perfect for longer group stays

## Booking Process for Groups

### Step 1: Initial Contact
- **Phone:** +46 46 33 36 00
- **Email:** info@placelund.se
- **Provide:** Dates, group size, purpose of visit

### Step 2: Requirements Discussion
- Number of rooms needed
- Type of accommodations (rooms/studios/apartments)
- Meeting space requirements
- Meal arrangements
- Special needs or requests

### Step 3: Quote & Proposal
- Customized package for your group
- Detailed pricing breakdown
- Room allocation plan
- Conference room scheduling

### Step 4: Confirmation
- Review and approve proposal
- Make deposit (if required)
- Receive confirmation details
- Finalize rooming list

### Step 5: Pre-Arrival
- Submit final attendee list
- Confirm meal counts
- Arrange check-in details
- Coordinate arrival times
