## Studio Overview

### What is a Hotel Studio?

Hotel studios at Place Lund offer a middle ground between traditional hotel rooms and full apartments:
- **Like a hotel room:** Weekly cleaning, hotel services, can book nightly
- **Like an apartment:** Kitchenette for self-catering, more space, independent living
- **Perfect balance:** Flexibility + services

---

## Studio Specifications

### Size & Layout
- **Size:** Approximately 25-30 m²
- **Layout:** Combined living/sleeping area with separate bathroom
- **Configuration:** Open plan with defined sleeping, living, and kitchen zones

### Sleeping Arrangements
- **Bed:** Queen or double bed
- **Capacity:** 1-2 people
- **Bedding:** Hilding Anders quality mattress
- **Allergy-friendly:** Down-free pillows and duvets

### Kitchen Facilities
**Kitchenette includes:**
- Stovetop/hotplate
- Refrigerator
- Microwave
- Coffee maker
- Sink
- Basic dishware and utensils
- Glasses and mugs
- Cutlery

**Note:** Limited compared to full apartment kitchen, but sufficient for basic meal preparation.

### Bathroom
- Private bathroom with shower
- Free toiletries provided
- Hair dryer
- Towels included

### Amenities
- Desk/workspace
- Free WiFi
- TV
- Wardrobe/closet space
- Safety box
- Parquet flooring
- No smoking

## Important Distinctions

### Meals NOT Included
**CRITICAL:** Hotel studios do NOT include breakfast or dinner, unlike traditional hotel rooms.

- **Traditional Hotel Rooms:** Breakfast + Dinner included ✅
- **Hotel Studios:** NO meals included ❌ (self-catering with kitchenette)
- **Apartments:** NO meals included ❌ (full kitchen)

### Booking Options
- **Nightly rentals:** Available
- **Weekly rentals:** Available
- **Monthly rentals:** Available
- **Minimum stay:** Typically 1 night (verify with hotel)
